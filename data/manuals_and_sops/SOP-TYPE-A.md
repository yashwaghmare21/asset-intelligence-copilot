# Standard Operating Procedure: Type A Well (Beam Pumping Unit)
**Document ID:** SOP-ENG-TYPE-A-REV4  
**Classification:** Mechanical Operations & Production Integrity  
**Applicable Equipment:** Conventional Beam Pump Jacks, Rod Lift Assemblies (Series 100-199)

## 1. Operating Principle & Production Thermal Criteria
Beam pumping units lift reservoir crude via reciprocating sucker rods. Crude extracted from the formation carries bottom-hole thermal energy to the surface wellhead manifold.

### Thermal Production Rule (Mandatory):
* **Production Status Determination:** A Type A well is classified as actively **PRODUCING** if and only if:
  $$\Delta T = T_{wellhead} - T_{ambient} \ge 15.0^\circ\text{F} \quad (8.33^\circ\text{C})$$
* **Non-Producing / Stopped Classification:**
  If $\Delta T < 15.0^\circ\text{F}$, the wellhead thermal signature indicates fluid stagnation or complete shutdown. The well is classified as **STOPPED** (or **SHUT IN** if officially logged as a planned suspension).
* **Weekly Inspection Downtime Rule:**
  Each routine weekly inspection finding $\Delta T < 15.0^\circ\text{F}$ logs exactly **7 days (1 week)** of cumulative operational downtime. If consecutive weekly inspections indicate non-production, each adds 7 days. Missed or skipped weekly inspections contribute **0 days** of downtime.

## 2. Typical Operating Parameters
* Normal Wellhead Temperature Range: $85^\circ\text{F} - 125^\circ\text{F}$
* Operating Pressure: $150 - 350\text{ psi}$
* Mechanical Stroke Rate: 6 to 12 strokes per minute (SPM)
