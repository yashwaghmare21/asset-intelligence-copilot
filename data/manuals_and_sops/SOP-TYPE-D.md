# Standard Operating Procedure: Type D Well (Progressive Cavity Pump - PCP)
**Document ID:** SOP-ENG-TYPE-D-REV5  
**Classification:** Viscous Heavy Crude Extraction  
**Applicable Equipment:** Positive Displacement Helical Rotor/Stator Units (Series 400-499)

## 1. Operating Principle & Production Thermal Criteria
Type D wells handle viscous crude with high sand or solids content. Friction and heavy crude lifting require strict temperature monitoring to avoid elastomer burnout.

### Thermal Production Rule (Mandatory):
* **Production Status Determination:** A Type D well is classified as actively **PRODUCING** if and only if:
  $$\Delta T = T_{wellhead} - T_{ambient} \ge 20.0^\circ\text{F} \quad (11.11^\circ\text{C})$$
* **Non-Producing / Stopped Classification:**
  If $\Delta T < 20.0^\circ\text{F}$, the rotor is stalled or suction is plugged. The well is classified as **STOPPED**.
* **Weekly Inspection Downtime Rule:**
  Each routine weekly inspection showing $\Delta T < 20.0^\circ\text{F}$ logs exactly **7 days (1 week)** of cumulative operational downtime. Missed inspections contribute **0 days**.
