# Standard Operating Procedure: Asset Repairs & Maintenance Accounting
**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-MAINT-REV2  
**Classification:** Field Maintenance, Workover & Cost Accounting  
**Applicable Equipment:** All Production Assets (Types A, B, C, D)

## 1. Repairs vs. Operational Downtime Accounting
A fundamental distinction governs the accounting of maintenance actions versus field availability metrics:

### Critical Accounting Rule:
* **Weekly Inspection Governs Downtime:** Official field downtime is computed solely through weekly thermal inspections. If an inspection confirms non-production, exactly **7 days (1 week)** is accrued.
* **Repair Time Duration:** Each repair ticket records the active maintenance duration (`time_stopped_hours` or days during repair) and the direct expenditure (`repair_cost_usd`). 
* **Independent Metric:** Maintenance job duration and workover hours must **not** be substituted for the weekly inspection downtime calculation. Workover logs explain the *underlying cause* ("why" a well was down), but weekly inspections quantify *how long* the well was down.

## 2. Planned Shutdowns ("Shut In")
* Wells taken offline deliberately for scheduled turnarounds, reservoir balancing, or safety upgrades are designated as **"Shut In"**.
* Operator notes recording planned shutdowns must be integrated into shared asset knowledge so that downtime during that period is attributed to planned maintenance rather than unannounced equipment failure.
